## Content To Fill Before Launch

Replace `[PLACEHOLDER]` sections with lawyer-reviewed Turkish text:

1. **Company legal name and address**
2. **MERSİS number** (if applicable)
3. **Contact email and phone**
4. **Return window** (default: 14 days per Turkish consumer law)
5. **Data controller** information for KVKK

- Seller identity (Dami Beauty)
- Product description and total price (shown in cart)
- Payment method (PayTR / credit card)
- Delivery time estimate
- Right of withdrawal (14-day return policy summary)
